Specifications

Quality in every detail

Branded fittings, durable finishes and safety built in — here's what goes into every home at Pacific Tower.

Structure & Walls

  • RCC framed structure
  • 150 mm external, 115 mm internal walls
  • 12 mm plain plaster inside, sand-faced plaster outside

Flooring

  • 2' × 2' vitrified tiles in all rooms
  • Anti-skid vitrified flooring in bathrooms

Doors & Windows

  • Steel main door with multi-level security
  • RCC framed laminated flush internal doors
  • Powder-coated aluminium windows with MS grills

Bathrooms

  • Ceramic wall tiles up to 7 ft
  • Jaquar / ESS ESS / Graffiti fittings
  • Aluminium louvre ventilation, geyser points (2.5 mm wiring)

Kitchen

  • Granite platform with stainless steel sink
  • 2 ft tiled dado
  • Points for fridge, microwave, mixer, aqua guard & washing machine

Electrical

  • Concealed wiring, ISI-marked modular switches
  • Anchor / Legrand or equivalent
  • AC points with 4 mm wiring, TV cable points in hall

Painting

  • Acrylic emulsion over two coats of putty
  • Waterproof emulsion on exterior walls

Lift & Safety

  • Kone, Johnson or equivalent lift
  • Waterproofing in all bathrooms & balconies
  • Steel railings on staircases
